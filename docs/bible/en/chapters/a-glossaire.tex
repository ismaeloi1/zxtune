\chapter{Glossary}
\begin{description}
  \item[ADSR] attack, decay, sustain, release: the four phases of a note envelope.
  \item[Cent] one hundredth of a semitone.
  \item[DDA] HuC6280 mode where the processor writes samples directly.
  \item[DSP] digital signal processor (echo, reverb).
  \item[FM] frequency modulation synthesis.
  \item[LFSR] feedback shift register producing noise.
  \item[Operator] sine oscillator of an FM voice.
  \item[PAL-N] Argentine video standard: clock close to NTSC, 50 frames per second.
  \item[Wavetable] stored waveform played in a loop.
  \item[Tracker] pattern-based composition software, and the format of the music it produces.
\end{description}
