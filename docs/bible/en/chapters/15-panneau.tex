\chapter{Live settings panel}
Changing an emulation setting, then going back to the track to hear it, is no way to compare: the ear forgets fast.
The live panel solves that. A long press on the oscilloscope, or the settings button, opens it over the
visualisation while the music keeps playing.

It shows only what concerns the current track: the settings of the chip being played, the oscilloscope's and the
general ones. You can switch from reSIDfp to reSID, or from one 6581 filter to another, and hear the difference
within a second. The few settings that need the track reloaded are marked ``applied to the next track''.
