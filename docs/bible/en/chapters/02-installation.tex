\chapter{Installation and first steps}

\section{Installing the application}
\soft is distributed as an APK file. Copy it to your phone or download it, then open it. Android asks you to allow
installation from that source: accept, this once. Updates install the same way, over the previous version; your
playlists and settings are kept.

\section{Permissions}
On first launch, \soft asks for access to your music. On recent Android versions you pick a specific folder rather
than giving access to the whole phone; we suggest choosing the folder that holds your collection. The notification
permission is used to show what is playing, and progress when you add a whole folder to a playlist.

\section{The three screens}
The application fits in three screens, reached from the bottom bar. The \textbf{Now playing} screen shows the
current track, the oscilloscope, the chip dashboard and the controls. The \textbf{Playlists} screen gathers your
playlists. The \textbf{Browser} screen gives access to your files and to online catalogues.

\capturedouble{now-playing-en}{playlist-en}{Now playing screen}{Playlists screen}

For your first listen, open the browser, choose a catalogue (the High Voltage SID Collection is a good place to
start) and tap a track. Playback starts and the Now playing screen fills up: the oscilloscope of each voice at the
top, the chip dashboard below. If you prefer an uncluttered screen, fold the information away with the arrow at the
bottom left.
