\chapter{Overview}

\section{What it is}
\soft is an Android music player dedicated to music of computers, consoles and arcade machines from the 1980s to the
2000s. This music is not recorded audio: it is programs and data driving a sound chip. To play it faithfully, \soft
reproduces the behaviour of these chips in software, which is emulation.

\section{Origin}
\soft is derived from \emph{ZXTune}, a free player created for ZX Spectrum music and later extended to many
platforms. \soft reuses its playback core and adds:
\begin{itemize}
  \item emulation engines chosen for accuracy (ares for the Super Nintendo, Ootake for the PC Engine, reSIDfp for the
        Commodore~64, Nuked for Yamaha FM chips);
  \item real-time visualisation of each voice and of the internal state of chips, chip by chip;
  \item a redesigned interface: multiple playlists, favourites, play-next queue, live settings.
\end{itemize}

\section{Features}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Plays \lhunaModulesCount{} music formats and opens \lhunaContainersCount{} container formats (archives,
        disk images, packers), see part~\ref{part:compat}.
  \item Browses the phone storage and large online catalogues.
  \item Shows one oscilloscope per voice, the SID filter response, and detailed dashboards for SID, FM, SNES,
        PC Engine, NES, MT-32 and Sega arcade PCM chips.
  \item Works with Android Auto and the lock screen.
\end{itemize}

\section{Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Android~4.1 or later for the full build.
  \item Oscilloscope: OpenGL ES, or Vulkan where available (smoother).
  \item About 16~MB for the application, plus your music.
\end{itemize}

\section{History}
\subsection{ZXTune}
ZXTune comes from the ZX Spectrum community, whose music scene produced tens of thousands of tunes for the
AY-3-8910 chip with trackers such as Sound Tracker, Pro Tracker or ASC Sound Master. Each tracker stored music in
its own format, and players of the time read only one. ZXTune, created by Vitamin, set out to read all of them, to
find them even inside disk images, archives or packed programs, and to replay them faithfully.

The project then opened to other platforms by integrating the best free libraries: libsidplayfp for the
Commodore~64, Game Music Emu for consoles, libopenmpt and libxmp for tracker modules, vgmstream for modern game
audio, libvgm for VGM logs. It exists as a command-line tool, a desktop GUI and an Android application.

\subsection{ZXTune\_LHUNA}
\soft is a branch of the ZXTune Android application with two goals:
\begin{description}
  \item[Fidelity] use for each chip the most accurate engine available, with a lighter engine selectable for slow
        devices and comparison;
  \item[Readability] show what each chip does, voice by voice and frame by frame, with views built around the
        synthesis of each chip rather than a generic view.
\end{description}
Everything displayed is the real state of the emulated chip at the current moment: no averaged or invented values.
Text does not repeat the graphics; it gives what graphics do not show: register values, notes, levels in dB.
