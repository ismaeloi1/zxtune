\chapter{Technical statistics}
Below the dashboard, a few technical lines describe what your phone is doing. You will only need them when something
goes wrong, but they quickly tell you where the problem is.
\begin{description}
  \item[scope] the number of lanes shown, the cost of triggering and the sync gap between sound and picture.
  \item[output] the output rate and audio buffer size. A larger buffer protects against dropouts at the cost of the
        picture lagging the sound slightly.
  \item[render] emulation speed relative to real time. This is the most useful line: at 300~\%, your phone computes
        three seconds of music per second; below 100~\%, it cannot keep up and the sound drops out.
  \item[vulkan / gles] the drawing engine, frames per second and the drawing time of each.
  \item[vk] the graphics processor and its driver version.
\end{description}
