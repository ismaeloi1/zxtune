\chapter{Playback}

\section{Modes}
\begin{description}
  \item[Shuffle] picks the next track at random within the playlist or folder.
  \item[Repeat] loops the current track indefinitely; otherwise it stops at the end of its known duration.
\end{description}

\section{Duration, loop and fade}
Much game music loops forever. \soft uses the known duration when the format or a database provides it (for SID, the
HVSC \emph{Songlengths} database), otherwise a configurable default duration. The progress bar marks the start of the
looped part when the track has an intro.

\section{Android Auto and lock screen}
Playback is controlled from the notification, the lock screen, Bluetooth headsets and Android Auto. Android Auto shows
playlists and a simplified visualisation.

\section{Sharing and ringtones}
The track menu shares the file, sends a link, creates a ringtone from an excerpt, and shows all properties of the
track.
