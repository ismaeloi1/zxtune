\chapter{Browser}

\section{Local sources}
The browser gives access to the phone storage and SD cards. On Android~10 and later, access goes through the system
folder picker: choose your music folder once and \soft remembers it.

\section{Online catalogues}
\soft accesses several public chip music catalogues live:
\begin{itemize}
  \item High Voltage SID Collection (Commodore~64);
  \item The Mod Archive, Modland and Amiga Music Preservation (tracker modules, Amiga);
  \item ZXArt and ZXTunes (ZX Spectrum), Aygor;
  \item Atari SAP Music Archive (Atari 8-bit);
  \item VGMRips (VGM logs of consoles and arcade machines);
  \item Joshw (console rips), Khinsider, OverClocked ReMix, Aminet, Scene.org;
  \item online radios.
\end{itemize}
\begin{description}
  \item[High Voltage SID Collection (HVSC)] the reference collection of Commodore~64 music, maintained by volunteers
        since the late 1990s; tens of thousands of SID files sorted by composer, plus the song length database used
        by \soft.
  \item[The Mod Archive, Modland, Amiga Music Preservation] the large tracker module archives: the first is
        community-run and rated by visitors, Modland sorts files by format, AMP documents Amiga musicians.
  \item[ZXArt, ZXTunes, Aygor] ZX Spectrum scene archives, including demoparty competitions.
  \item[ASMA] Atari SAP Music Archive, the HVSC counterpart for Atari 8-bit machines.
  \item[VGMRips] VGM logs of console and arcade games, full packs per game.
  \item[Joshw, Khinsider] console rips (NSF, SPC, GBS, PSF\dots) and game soundtracks.
  \item[OverClocked ReMix] modern arrangements of game music.
  \item[Aminet, Scene.org] historical Amiga and demoscene archives.
\end{description}
Catalogues are third-party services: their content, availability and terms depend on their owners. \soft queries
them live and caches files already played.

\section{Archives and multi-song files}
\soft opens archives (ZIP, RAR, 7z, LHA\dots) and disk images (TR-DOS, SCL\dots) as folders. A file holding several
songs (SID, NSF, AY, KSS\dots) is played directly instead: its subsongs play in sequence, then playback continues with
the next file of the folder.

\section{Search and selection}
The magnifier filters the current folder. A long press starts multiple selection; the top bar then offers:
\begin{description}
  \item[Add] asks for the target playlist and adds the files (folders are scanned entirely);
  \item[Play next] puts the selection in the play queue (section~\ref{sec:file}).
\end{description}
