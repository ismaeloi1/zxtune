\chapter{The browser}

\section{Your files}
The browser opens the phone's storage and SD cards. On Android~10 and later, access goes through the system folder
picker: you point once at your music folder and \soft remembers it.

\soft opens archives (ZIP, RAR, 7z, LHA\dots) and disk images (TR-DOS, SCL\dots) as folders: you do not need to
unpack them. It also finds music hidden in packed programs or machine snapshots, which is common in ZX Spectrum scene
collections.

A file holding several tracks (SID, NSF, AY, KSS\dots) plays directly when you tap it: its subsongs follow one
another, then playback continues with the next file in the folder.

\section{Online catalogues}
The browser also gives live access to large public collections. The \textbf{High Voltage SID Collection} gathers
nearly all Commodore~64 music, sorted by composer, and provides the duration database we use. \textbf{The Mod
Archive}, \textbf{Modland} and \textbf{Amiga Music Preservation} cover Amiga and PC tracker modules; Modland sorts
them by format, AMP documents the musicians. \textbf{ZXArt}, \textbf{ZXTunes} and \textbf{Aygor} archive the ZX
Spectrum scene, demo-party competitions included. \textbf{ASMA} is the HVSC of 8-bit Atari. \textbf{VGMRips} offers
VGM recordings of console and arcade games, whole game at a time. \textbf{Joshw} and \textbf{Khinsider} offer
console rips and soundtracks, \textbf{OverClocked ReMix} reinterpretations, \textbf{Aminet} and \textbf{Scene.org}
the historical archives of the Amiga and the demoscene. A few online radios complete the list.

These catalogues are independent services: their content and availability depend on the people who run them. Tracks
you have already played stay in the cache and play again without a connection.

\section{Searching and selecting}
The magnifier filters the folder shown. To act on several items, long-press the first then tap the others. The
``Add'' button asks which playlist to file them in; folders are scanned in full, which can take a while for a large
collection (progress shows in the notifications). The ``Play next'' button puts the selection in the queue, to hear
it right after the current track.
