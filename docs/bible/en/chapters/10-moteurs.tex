\chapter{Emulation engines}

\section{Commodore 64: reSID and reSIDfp}
Both engines come from the libsidplayfp library, which also emulates the Commodore~64 processor and components needed
to run the original players.
\begin{description}
  \item[reSIDfp] (default) floating-point model of the analogue filter and of the combined waveforms;
  \item[reSID] historical model, lighter.
\end{description}
Chip model (6581 or 8580), filter curves and bias are configurable (chapter~\ref{chap:emu-settings}).

\section{Super Nintendo: ares or snes\_spc}
\begin{description}
  \item[ares] (default) cycle-accurate emulation of the SPC700 processor and the S-DSP, taken from the ares emulator;
        stereo is rendered as on the console, phase inversions included;
  \item[snes\_spc] historical engine by Shay Green (blargg), lighter.
\end{description}
The same setting applies to SPC files and to SNSF rips, which also run the main 65816 processor.

\section{PC Engine: Ootake}
The HuC6280 is emulated by the core of Ootake, a Japanese PC Engine emulator known for its fidelity. \soft uses it for
VGM logs and reads the wavetable of each voice from it for the dashboard.

\section{Sega Saturn and arcade: SCSP}
The SCSP is emulated with its effects DSP enabled: reverb and effects programmed by the game are audible.

\section{Engine authors}
\begin{description}
  \item[reSID] Dag Lem, from measurements of real chips; the historical reference of SID emulation.
  \item[reSIDfp] Antti Lankila then Leandro Nini, maintainer of libsidplayfp; floating-point filter model and
        sampled combined waveforms.
  \item[ares] Near and the ares team, successors of bsnes and higan, known for cycle accuracy.
  \item[snes\_spc] Shay Green (blargg), also author of Game Music Emu.
  \item[Nuked] Alexey Khokholov (nukeykt), emulators written from the study of the Yamaha chips' silicon.
  \item[ymfm] Aaron Giles, Yamaha FM emulation written for MAME.
  \item[Genesis Plus GX] Eke-Eke, Mega Drive emulator; its YM2612 core is fast.
  \item[Ootake] Kitao Nakamura, PC Engine emulator.
  \item[Munt] the Munt project, Roland MT-32 emulator.
\end{description}

\section{Accuracy and cost}
On a recent phone all engines run well above real time. The most expensive are Nuked OPM and Nuked OPN2, then ares
for the Super Nintendo; the lightest are Genesis Plus GX, MAME and snes\_spc. The ``render'' statistics line shows the
emulation speed: above 100~\%, the device keeps up.
