\chapter{Emulation engines}

For several chips there are several emulators, more or less accurate and more or less demanding. We picked the most
faithful one as the default engine, and kept a lighter alternative where one exists. If your phone struggles (the
``render'' line of the statistics drops below 100~\%), change engine in the settings.

\section{Commodore 64: reSIDfp and reSID}
Both engines come from the libsidplayfp library, which also emulates the Commodore~64 processor and the circuits the
original players need to run. reSID is Dag Lem's historical model, built from measurements of real chips. reSIDfp,
developed later by Antti Lankila then Leandro Nini, models the analogue filter in floating point and reproduces
combined waveforms from samples of real chips; it is our default engine.

\section{Super Nintendo: ares and snes\_spc}
The ares engine, from the work of Near and their team on bsnes and higan, reproduces the SPC700 and the S-DSP cycle
by cycle. It renders stereo exactly as the console does, phase inversions included: some tracks therefore sound wider
than in other players, and that is expected. snes\_spc, written by Shay Green (blargg), is lighter and remains
available. The same setting applies to SPC and SNSF.

\section{Yamaha FM chips}
For each FM family you choose the core:
\begin{longtable}{@{}l l p{0.5\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Family} & \textbf{Chips} & \textbf{Cores} \\ \midrule
OPN2 & YM2612, YM3438 & Nuked (default), ymfm, Genesis Plus GX \\
OPM & YM2151 & Nuked (default), ymfm, MAME \\
OPN & YM2203, YM2608, YM2610 & ymfm (default), MAME \\
OPL & YM3526, YM3812, YMF262 & Nuked (default), AdLibEmu, MAME \\
OPLL & YM2413 & Nuked (default), EMU2413, MAME \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
Alexey Khokholov's Nuked cores were written from a study of the chips' silicon; they are the most exact and the most
expensive. ymfm, by Aaron Giles, is the emulation MAME uses. Eke-Eke's Genesis Plus GX and MAME's older cores are
fast.

\section{PC Engine: Ootake}
We emulate the HuC6280 with the core of Ootake, Kitao Nakamura's PC Engine emulator, known for its fidelity to the
hardware. It is also from this core that we read each voice's wavetable for the PC Engine view.

\section{Saturn and Sega arcade: SCSP}
The SCSP is emulated with its effects processor on. We turned it on by default because the music of these machines
was mixed with its reverb: without it, it sounds dry and empty.

\section{What accuracy costs}
On a recent phone every engine runs well above real time. The most demanding are Nuked OPM and Nuked OPN2, then ares;
the lightest are Genesis Plus GX, the MAME cores and snes\_spc.
