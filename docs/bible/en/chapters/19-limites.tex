\chapter{Known limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Android Auto shows at most 32 lanes.
  \item Saturn SSF and PlayStation PSF files have no detailed per-voice view yet; their oscilloscope shows the main
        output.
  \item The PC Engine view is available for VGM logs; HES files use the generic view.
  \item Settings marked ``applied from the next track'' do not change the current track.
\end{itemize}
