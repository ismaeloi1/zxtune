\chapter{Development cost estimate}\label{chap:chiffrage}

\section{Scope}
This chapter estimates the cost of developing \soft with a team of developers, that is what the same work would cost
when done conventionally. It covers the work of this branch, measured in the repository between 2 October and
24 November 2026.

\textbf{The cost of the proprietary NebulaWeave drivers is not included and is not disclosed in this document, as a
trade secret.}

\section{Measurement}
Added lines are counted per area, excluding data and binary resources. Third-party code is split in two:
\emph{imported} code taken as is (ymfm, mt32emu, adrenotools, ares SPC700 and S-DSP cores, sidid base) and
\emph{adapted} code (extensions added to cores for per-voice outputs and states, standalone port of ares, SID engine
options).

\begin{longtable}{@{}l r l@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Area} & \textbf{Lines} & \textbf{Kind} \\ \midrule
ZXTune native core (C++) & 22\,700 & created \\
Android application (Kotlin, Java) & 30\,400 & created \\
Android resources (XML) & 6\,200 & created \\
Android tests & 7\,200 & created \\
Desktop version (Qt) & 7\,700 & created \\
VGM extraction tools (Model 2/3) & 4\,100 & created \\
Third-party libraries, adapted & about 7\,000 & adapted \\
Third-party libraries, imported & about 24\,000 & integrated \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Productivity assumptions}
Rates are averages of comparable projects, tests and debugging included:
\begin{longtable}{@{}l r@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Kind of work} & \textbf{Lines per day} \\ \midrule
Emulation and signal processing (C++) & 75 \\
Adapting third-party cores (reading, measuring, extending) & 60 \\
Interface and application logic (Kotlin) & 150 \\
XML resources & 400 \\
Tests & 200 \\
Desktop interface (Qt) & 120 \\
Tools & 80 \\
Integrating imported code (license, build, wiring) & 1\,000 \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
Imported code is not costed like written code: only its integration is counted. Adapted code requires understanding
the existing core before adding a few hundred lines, hence the low rate.
